Food Poisoning: When to Go To The Emergency Room - St ...Food poisoning (foodborne illness) is caused by eating contaminated food or drinks with bacteria, viruses, parasites, or toxins, leading to symptoms like nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and stomach pain, usually starting within hours but sometimes days later, with most cases resolving in a few days through rest and hydration, though severe symptoms like high fever, bloody stools, or dehydration require immediate medical attention. Key prevention involves proper food handling: keeping food cold, clean, and hot, and ensuring thorough cooking.
