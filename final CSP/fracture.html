Fracture Care : Immediate first aid for fractures focuses on preventing further injury, controlling bleeding, and reducing pain until medical help arrives.

Fracture care involves stabilizing the injured area, applying a cold pack, and ensuring the person is safe and calm. If bones are visibly out of place, bleeding is heavy, or the fracture involves the head, spine, or pelvis, call emergency medical services immediately.

First Aid Steps:
• Apply pressure with a clean cloth to stop any bleeding.
• Immobilize the injured area using a splint (rolled magazine, cardboard, or SAM splint).
• Tie the splint just enough to hold — not too tight.
• Apply a cold pack wrapped in a towel for 15–20 minutes to reduce swelling.
• Elevate the limb if possible and not painful.

Treat for Shock:
Lay the person down, keep them warm, and slightly elevate their legs unless the injury is to the back, hip, or leg.

Pain Management: Mild pain relievers such as paracetamol can be given, if available.

Do Not:
• Do not move the person unnecessarily.
• Do not try to straighten the bone.
• Do not give food or drinks (they may need surgery).

Check Circulation:
Look for numbness, pale skin, cold fingers/toes, or tingling. Seek help immediately if circulation seems blocked.
