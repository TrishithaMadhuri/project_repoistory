NoseBleeding

A nosebleed, or epistaxis, is bleeding from the nose, often caused by dry air, nose-picking, injury, or overuse of nasal sprays. To stop a common, anterior nosebleed, sit down, lean forward, and pinch the soft part of your nose for 10 to 15 minutes while breathing through your mouth. While most nosebleeds are minor, seek medical attention for severe or prolonged bleeding.
