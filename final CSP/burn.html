Cool the burn: Hold the affected area under cool (not cold) running water for 10–20 minutes.

Remove tight items: Remove rings or tight items before swelling starts.

Cover the burn: Loosely wrap with a clean bandage.

Apply lotion: Use aloe vera or cocoa butter after cooling.

Pain relief: Ibuprofen or acetaminophen helps reduce pain.

Do NOT: Do not break blisters, apply ice, butter, or unknown ointments.
