Bleeding is severe or you cannot stop it after 15-20 minutes of continuous pressure. The wound is deep, appears to be more than half an inch long, or you cannot clean it thoroughly. There is something stuck in the wound, like an impaled object. The injury was caused by an animal or human bite, an electrical injury, or a puncture. The wound is on the face or neck. For severe, life-threatening bleeding: Call emergency services immediately. While waiting for help, apply direct pressure and take steps to prevent shock by laying the person flat, raising their feet about 12 inches, and covering them with a blanket. Do not move the person if you suspect a head, neck, back, or leg injury. .
