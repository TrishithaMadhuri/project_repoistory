Choking

Choking happens when something blocks the airway, stopping you from breathing, often by lodging in the throat or windpipe, causing inability to speak, cough, or breathe, and potentially turning skin blue; it's a medical emergency requiring immediate first aid, such as back blows and abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver), to dislodge the object and restore oxygen, preventing brain damage or death. Prevention includes cutting food small, keeping small items from children, and chewing thoroughly
