Cough&cold

To relieve a cold and cough, rest and drink plenty of fluids like water, juice, or tea. Use home remedies like gargling with warm salt water, using a humidifier, or trying honey in warm tea. Over-the-counter medicines can help, but read labels carefully and consult a doctor before giving them to children or if symptoms worsen. Seek medical advice if symptoms are severe or don't improve after 10 days.
