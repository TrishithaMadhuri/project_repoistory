Dizziness is a common feeling of being disoriented, lightheaded, or like you or your surroundings are spinning (vertigo). Causes can range from simple issues like dehydration or getting up too quickly to more serious problems like inner ear infections, cardiovascular issues, or neurological conditions. You should see a doctor if dizziness is severe, frequent, or accompanied by other symptoms like chest pain, a severe headache, or trouble speaking.
