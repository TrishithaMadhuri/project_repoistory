Anemia

Anemia is a condition where the body doesn't have enough healthy red blood cells to carry adequate oxygen to tissues, causing symptoms like tiredness, weakness, and shortness of breath. It can result from factors like nutritional deficiencies (especially iron, vitamin B12, and folate), blood loss, chronic diseases, or inherited disorders like sickle cell anemia. Treatment depends on the type and cause and can include supplements, dietary changes, or other medical interventions.
